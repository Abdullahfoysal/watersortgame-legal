Glassly: Water Sort Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Developer: Gayen BD ("we", "us")

Contact: gayenbd@gmail.com

App: Glassly: Water Sort Puzzle for Android (package com.gayenbd.waterpouring)

1. Who we are and what this app is

Glassly: Water Sort Puzzle is a single-player water-sort puzzle game. It works offline. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads and no in-app purchases in this version. This policy explains what information the app sends to us and to Google, why, and what choices you have.

2. What we collect

Your game progress (levels, coins, settings, daily streaks) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

The app uses Google's Firebase services. When you use the app, these services send the following to Google servers on our behalf:

  • Device and app-instance identifiers. A Firebase installation ID, an app-instance ID, and a Crashlytics installation ID. They identify one installation of the app, not you as a person.
  • Advertising ID. The app declares the Android advertising ID permission because Firebase Analytics can read it. In this version we use it for analytics only. We do not show ads and do not use it to personalise anything. See section 6 for how to reset or delete it.
  • In-game events (app activity). For example: app opened, level started, level completed, moves, stars, time taken, undo/hint/extra-tube used, daily challenge started or completed, streak events, theme in use, and which test group (A/B cohort) you are in.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. Stack traces and device state when the app crashes: device make and model, Android version, app version, language, free memory/disk, orientation, and whether the app was in the foreground.
  • Approximate location. Google derives country/region/city-level location from your IP address. We never ask for GPS or precise location, and the app does not request location permission.
  • Remote Config data. To download our game settings, Firebase receives the app ID and version, Android version, country code, language and time zone.

3. What we do NOT collect

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location, messages, payment or financial information, health data. We do not create accounts. We do not sell your data. We do not enable Google Signals, so analytics data is not linked to your Google account.

4. Why we use it

  • Analytics: to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard) and to improve it.
  • Crash reporting and diagnostics: to find and fix bugs.
  • App functionality: Remote Config lets us adjust game settings (for example difficulty or free undo count) without an app update.

We do not use this data for advertising or profiling, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service provider below.

Google acts as our service provider (processor) for this data under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. Google may also use certain Firebase service data as described in its own policy.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID (Android). Open Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices Settings > Google > Ads) and choose "Delete advertising ID" (Android 12 and later) or "Reset advertising ID". You can also turn off ads personalisation there. Menu names vary by device. After deletion, the app receives a blank ID.
  • Uninstall. Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device. Analytics collection stops.
  • Clear app data. Settings > Apps > Glassly: Water Sort Puzzle > Storage > Clear data removes local progress and resets the app-instance ID.
  • In-app switch. Open Settings > Privacy and turn off "Share analytics & crash reports". Analytics and crash collection then stops and the app sends no further analytics events or crash reports. The switch is on by default and your choice is remembered on your device.
  • Access, deletion and other rights. Email gayenbd@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, or to request deletion. Because we hold only an app-instance ID and not your identity, we may ask you to send your Support ID (Settings > About > Support ID > Copy; it is shown only while analytics sharing is on and the app is online) so we can locate and delete your analytics records. If you live in the EEA, UK, California or another place with privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will respond within the time required by law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Analytics event-level data: 2 months (Google Analytics lets us choose 2 or 14 months). Aggregated reports without user identifiers may be kept longer.
  • Crashlytics crash traces and identifiers: 90 days (Firebase default).
  • Remote Config and other installation IDs: removed within 180 days after we send a deletion request to Firebase.
  • Local game data: until you uninstall or clear app data.

8. Children

Glassly: Water Sort Puzzle is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact gayenbd@gmail.com and we will delete the related data. Where local law sets a higher minimum age, the app is not intended for people below that age.

9. Security

Data sent from the app to Firebase travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Google applies its own security measures to data it stores. No system is perfectly secure.

10. International transfers

Google runs servers in many countries, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live, including the United States. Google states that it applies the same protections wherever data is processed and complies with the EU-U.S., Swiss-U.S. and UK Extension Data Privacy Frameworks. Where required, transfers rely on approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add ads or in-app purchases. The effective date at the top will change, and for significant changes we will say so in the app or the store listing.
